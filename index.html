What this page is about

Feelings, without the awkwardness.

KIPM Confession is designed as a fun, respectful college community. The goal is connection — not pressure, embarrassment, harassment or public exposure.

🔒

Privacy first

❤️

☕

Good vibes only

Community rules

Keep it cute. Keep it safe.

These rules apply to every confession, comment, story, collaboration and interaction connected with KIPM Confession.

Same campus.
Different stories. ♥

Anonymous does not mean anything goes. Protect other students just as you would want your own privacy protected.

01

Be respectful

No abusive, insulting, humiliating or degrading content about another student.

02

No harassment

Do not repeatedly target, follow, pressure or threaten someone through the page.

03

Respect “No”

A rejection is a complete answer. Never pressure someone to respond, meet or date.

04

No private information

No phone numbers, home addresses, passwords, private screenshots, personal IDs or other sensitive information.

05

No sexual or explicit content

Keep content suitable for a college community. Explicit, exploitative or sexual content is not allowed.

06

No impersonation

Do not pretend to be another student, faculty member, administrator or the page team.

07

No false accusations

Do not use the page to spread unverified allegations, rumors or claims that could harm someone.

08

No bullying or discrimination

Hateful or discriminatory content based on identity, background, appearance or personal characteristics is prohibited.

09

No pressure for a meetup

Coffee requests should always be optional. Never reveal or demand someone’s location.

10

Admin moderation

The page team may reject, edit for safety, restrict or remove content that breaks these rules.

11

No doxxing

Never share addresses, live locations, timetables, class schedules, vehicle details or identifying information.

12

No stalking

Do not post routines, locations or instructions intended to track another student.

13

No threats or blackmail

Threats to expose chats, photos, secrets or personal information are strictly prohibited.

14

No fake or misleading media

Do not edit photos, screenshots, audio or video to falsely target or humiliate someone.

15

No spam

Repeated submissions, copied posts, promotions and engagement bait may be rejected.

16

No academic misconduct

Do not arrange cheating, leak exam material or impersonate faculty through this page.

17

No sexual or exploitative content

Keep the community college-appropriate; never sexualize minors or share intimate content.

18

Respect faculty and staff

No targeted humiliation, harassment or inappropriate speculation about faculty or staff.

19

Consent before sharing

Get permission before posting identifiable photos, screenshots or private conversations.

20

One “no” means stop

If someone asks you to stop or remove content, respect the boundary and do not escalate.

Terms & safety

Simple terms for a better community.

Use of the KIPM Confession community means agreeing to the following principles.

Anonymous ≠ guaranteed identity protection

The page can avoid publicly displaying a sender's identity, but no online system should be described as absolutely risk-free. Do not submit information you would not want handled by a page admin.

Content is moderated

Not every submission has to be published. Safety, relevance, respect and privacy come before engagement or entertainment.

Consent matters

A person does not owe anyone a reply, meeting, relationship or explanation. Respect boundaries immediately.

Report serious issues

For threats, stalking, blackmail, intimate-image abuse or immediate danger, do not rely on an Instagram page. Contact appropriate college authorities or emergency services.

No fake engagement

This website intentionally does not display fabricated confessions, fake testimonials, fake student identities or manufactured comments.

College affiliation

Unless officially authorized, this community page should not claim to represent KIPM College administration. Make the independent/student-run status clear on the Instagram profile.

Important: Never post someone's private information or intimate content without consent. If a situation involves threats, coercion, stalking or immediate danger, seek help from trusted people and appropriate authorities.
Posting standards

Before anything goes public.

Keep the page fun without turning another student's private life into content.

✅ Good to post

  • Respectful crushes and appreciation
  • Friendly, pressure-free coffee invitations
  • Positive campus memories and harmless humor
  • Messages that do not expose private information

❌ Not suitable

  • Phone numbers, addresses or live locations
  • Private chats, intimate images or secrets
  • Threats, insults, rumors or accusations
  • Posts meant to embarrass or pressure someone

🛡️ Admin decision

Moderators may reject content when safety, privacy or respect is a concern. A safe rewrite may be requested.

🌱 Golden rule

Ask: “Would I be comfortable if this were about me?” If not, do not post it.

Quick FAQ

Questions students may have.

Is KIPM Confession officially run by the college?

Only say it is official if you have authorization. Otherwise clearly describe it as an independent/student-run community page.

Can someone be forced to reply to a confession?

No. A confession is not a demand for attention, friendship, a date or a relationship. Respect a person's choice.

Can private screenshots be posted?

Not without consent. Private conversations and personal media should stay private.

What happens to unsafe submissions?

They should be rejected or removed. Serious threats, blackmail, stalking or immediate danger should be taken to appropriate authorities rather than handled through public posts.

Can the page post fake confessions for engagement?

No. This website and the community concept are intentionally built around genuine, moderated participation rather than fabricated student messages.

🛡️

Safety beats virality.

If a post gets attention but puts someone at risk, it is not worth publishing. Protect privacy first, engagement second.
